01 — About

Engineering, end to end.

I'm a second-year Computer Engineering student at Queen's University in Kingston, Ontario. Most of what I do is embedded: calibrating a VTOL airframe on a PX4 flight controller, designing the protection circuitry that keeps a high-voltage bus safe, and getting sensors and radios to agree with each other. Then comes the software — ROS 2 nodes in Docker, MATLAB/Simulink flight models, and computer-vision pipelines. I like working both ends: the part that touches metal, and the part that tells it what to do.
